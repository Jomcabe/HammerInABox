How the Hammer Drops

Five stages of escalation, from passive detection to verbal annihilation.

01 DETECT
The ultrasonic sensor pings continuously, watching for a person to enter range.		 02 STATUS
Arduino lights up the LCD: target locked, system armed, scanning sequence engaged.		 03 CAPTURE
The Arduino signals the laptop, which fires up the external camera and snaps a frame.
04 ROAST
Gemini identifies the subject and generates a roast in the voice of the Terminator.		 05 DELIVER
Text-to-speech reads the roast. Then Brian Griffin barks. Target retreats.		 it works on my laptop
